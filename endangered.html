One or two sentences introducing this organism for a Year 10 reader. What is it and why did you choose it?

Structural adaptation: Name of adaptation

Describe a structural adaptation: a physical feature of the body.

Explain how this feature increases fitness, meaning how it helps the organism survive and reproduce.

Physiological adaptation: Name of adaptation

Describe a physiological adaptation: something that happens inside the body, such as how it digests, regulates temperature or conserves water.

Explain how this increases fitness.

Behavioural adaptation: Name of adaptation

Describe a behavioural adaptation: something the organism does, such as when it is active, how it feeds, hides, moves or interacts with others.

Explain how this increases fitness.

Natural habitat

Where does it live in the wild? Describe the habitat and its geographic range.

Environmental challenges

What key challenges does it face in its natural environment? Think about climate, food and water, predators, disease and human impacts.

Conservation program

Describe a conservation program involving this species. Who runs it, what do they do (e.g. breeding, release, habitat protection) and what has it achieved?

A bioethical issue

Identify a bioethical issue connected to the conservation program. State it as a question people could disagree on.

Applying an ethical concept

Name the ethical concept you're using and define it briefly.

Use that concept to analyse the issue. Consider different perspectives, then state your group's position and the reasoning behind it.
